Site-to-Site IPsec VPN tunnel between Branch (Tunnel0, MTU 1400) and HQ.

[SYMPTOM DESCRIPTION]

[GROUND TRUTH / EXPECTED ROOT CAUSE]
Large UDP DNS responses exceed tunnel MTU 1400 with DF (Don't Fragment) bit set; 'no ip unreachables' suppresses ICMP Fragmentation Needed, creating a PMTU black hole.
